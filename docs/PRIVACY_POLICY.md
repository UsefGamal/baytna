# Baytna privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Baytna is a household budgeting app. This page explains what it stores and where.

## Without sync (the default)

Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, recurring items, names) is stored only on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. Backup and CSV files are saved where you choose and only leave the phone if you move them.

## With sync turned on

If you turn on sync, Baytna uses Google Firebase:

- **Firebase Authentication** stores your email address and a password hash so you can sign in.
- **Cloud Firestore** stores your household's data so the members' phones stay in step. Only signed-in members of the household can read or change it; security rules enforce this on the server.

Data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it as our service provider under the [Firebase terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms). We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

## Notifications

Reminders are optional and are worked out on your phone. No push server is involved.

## Deleting your data

- Data on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage.
- Cloud data and your account: email the support address listed on the Play Store page from your account's email address. We delete the account and its household data within 30 days.

## Children

Baytna is meant for adults managing a household and is not directed at children.

## Contact

Questions: the support email on the app's Play Store page.
