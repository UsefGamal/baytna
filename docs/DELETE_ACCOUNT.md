# Delete your Baytna account

Baytna works without an account. An account exists only if you turned on sync. Deleting it removes your sign-in and your link to the shared household.

## In the app

1. Open **More → Cloud sync**.
2. Tap **Delete my cloud account**.
3. Enter your password and tap **Delete account**.

What happens:

- Your sign-in (email and password) is deleted from Firebase Authentication.
- If your partner is still in the household, you leave it and they keep the shared data.
- If you are the last member, the household's data in the cloud (accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, recurring items, activity and invites) is deleted.
- Sync turns off. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email the support address on the Play Store page **from your account's email address** and ask for your Baytna account to be deleted. We delete the sign-in, and the household's cloud data if no other member remains, within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Nothing is kept after deletion: there are no backups of cloud data on our side.
